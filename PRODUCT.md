# Portfolio product contract

This product presents Ramin's professional work to research/university and technology-company audiences. The owner explicitly requests interactive, narrative-led design rather than static résumé sheets. The site is a web portfolio with an authenticated CV source and a gallery of alternate presentations.

All personal information comes through the existing canonical CV adapter. Views consume the normalized model; future source/schema changes belong at that boundary. Preserve all professional records and resource links, the portrait, readable complete detail, and existing templates. Do not invent accomplishments, proficiency percentages, research results, scientific relationships or performance metrics. Illustrative scientific artwork is conceptual.

The primary Synthesis presentation has three perspectives: integrated, biology/bioinformatics, and programming/machine learning. Perspective changes presentation and emphasis without deleting records. Additional experiences may emphasize chemistry or a narrower domain while retaining access to the complete portfolio.

## Platform and stack

Web, desktop and mobile. Existing plain JavaScript/CSS portfolio, normalized JSON/API boundary, PostgreSQL persistence and a static-export Next.js collection. Preserve the existing authorized GitHub and Vercel project. Private data, credentials and real screenshots remain outside public Git. Public captures and tests use synthetic data.

## Interaction requirements

Keyboard-operable controls, readable light/dark themes, mobile layouts, explicit motion preferences, manual alternatives to scroll effects, and source-backed search/details. Changes should remain immediately usable during animation. The owner delegates design decisions and implementation orchestration and prefers focused investigation and batched verification to minimize unnecessary usage.

This document records explicit conversation requirements; it introduces no new product commitments.
